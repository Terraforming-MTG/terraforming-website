General Information

Terraforming requires a minimum of 2 players, but it can be played with more.
The starting life total in Terraforming is 20.
The minimum deck size is 60 cards. There is no maximum deck size.
Only lands are allowed, with the exception of a single card, known as the Joker.

Deck Constraints

The minimum deck size for a Terraforming deck is 60 cards. A Terraforming deck may not contain more than 4 copies of a card. The only exceptions to this rule are basic lands. You may have any amount of basic lands in your deck.
A Terraforming deck may not contain more than one copy of a restricted card.
A Terraforming deck may only contain land cards, with the exception of a singular non-land card designated the Joker.
A Terraforming deck contains a card known as the Mainland.
Two-sided cards that are lands on one side are allowed to be played in a Terraforming deck without taking up the Joker spot.
All un-set cards are legal unless stated otherwise.

The Joker

The Joker is a card of any type in a Terraforming deck.
All cards in a Terraforming deck must be a land, excluding the Joker.
The Joker may be a land card.
The Joker can be from an un-set and it can be restricted.

Mainland

At the beginning of the game, you designate one land card in your library as your Mainland. There may only be one copy of the card you choose as your Mainland.
After a hand has been kept, you search your library for your mainland and put it into your hand. You then shuffle a differnt card from your hand into your library.
If the Mainland card is in your hand after when you keep your hand, skip this step.

Gameplay Rules

If a rule is not stated within this page, follow the rules of standard-edition Magic: The Gathering.

Players may play additional lands each turn. For each land a player would play after their first, they must pay an additional mana cost correlating to the amount of lands they have played this turn.

Etc...

Lands put onto the battlefield through card effects like Evolving Wilds do not count toward the additional land count and do not require the additional mana cost to be payed.

The first land a player plays in a game enters with an indestructible counter. As long as the indestructible counter is on that land, effects that say destroy don't affect it.

Banned and Restricted cards

Cards included on the Terraforming banlist cannot be included in a Terraforming deck.
There may only be one copy of cards on the Terraforming restricted list included in a Terraforming deck.
These lists can be found here.

Rule Authority

If a standard-edition Magic: The Gathering rule conflicts with a Terraforming rule, the Terraforming rule takes precedence.
If a rule is changed or added, existing decks must be changed to coincide with the current rules.
If a rule or statement somewhere else on this website contradicts something stated on this page, the rules stated on this page take precedence.
