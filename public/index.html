What is Terraforming?

Terraforming is a custom Magic: The Gathering format that provides a unique deck-building experience with land-focused gameplay.

A terraforming deck must contain at least 60 cards (but it may contain more).
A terraforming deck may only contain lands, with the exception of a single card designated the Joker, which can be any card type.
After keeping an opening hand, each player searches their library for their Mainland card and puts it into their hand. They then shuffle a card from their hand into their library.
Your first land played each game receives an indestructible counter.
You may play additional lands by paying an increasing cost starting with for each land played beyond your first.
